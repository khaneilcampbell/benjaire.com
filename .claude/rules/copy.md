# Visible copy rules

- Company voice: "Benjaire" and "we", never an individual.
- Say "a New York company". US spelling.
- No em dashes and no hyphenated compounds in visible copy.
- Meta descriptions are 120 to 160 characters.
- Reuse `SITE.description` and `SITE.boilerplate` from `site/src/site.ts` word for word.
- Nothing may imply brands, partners, customers, results or capabilities that do not exist. Brands appear only after public approval, via `site/src/content/brands/`.
- Supplier copy uses only "exploring" and "interested in": no MOQs, partner names or capability claims.
- `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/digital/flow-thanks` stay `noindex`; the legal pages stay out of the sitemap until attorney review is complete.
- New copy is a draft for owner review.
